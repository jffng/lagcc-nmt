## **Course Description**

We will look at the fundamental principles of web design, focusing on user experience and visual design for websites and mobile applications. Topics include typography, visual/graphic design, layout and responsive design, motion and interaction design, and accessibility. We will start by doing research.

We will show each other some fundamental techniques of interacting with computers, and ask you to experiment and try some of them. If you like them, you can use it in your own interface.

## **Goals**

The course has three goals:

1. Understand foundational web design practices and principles
2. Practice with professional design tools and workflows
3. Design an interface to a project of one's choosing

## **Objectives**

On completion of this course, you will be able to:

- Lay out typography in a readable and expressive manner
- Apply color, composition, and visual hierarchy to a digital design
- Prototype motion and interaction for an interface
- Evaluate and remediate a design for accessibility
