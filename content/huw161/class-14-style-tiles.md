### Assignment 3: Visual Design

## Requirements

Develop three visual designs for your product, then apply one of them to your wireframes on desktop and mobile.

**1. Three directions.** Each direction includes:

- **Mood board:** 3 to 5 reference images that set the mood.
- **Style palette:**
    - Heading and body typography: one display typeface and one body typeface.
    - Brand palette: primary, secondary and tertiary colors.
    - UI color palette: background, surface, foreground, muted and accent, derived from your brand colors.

**2. Visual Design:** choose one direction and apply it to your existing wireframes: one desktop screen and one mobile screen.

## What to submit

- **Figma link:** a link to your Figma file, shared so your instructor can view it, containing all three visual directions.
- **Exported PNGs:** two images of your chosen direction, one desktop screen and one mobile screen.
- **Written explanation:** 2 to 3 sentences on your design choices. Say why the direction suits your product and audience, and point to specific choices in type or color.

## Rubric

The assignment is worth 100 points. Each row earns full, half or no points.

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|Criterion|Points|Full credit looks like|
|Mood boards|15|Three distinct directions, each with 3 to 5 references that clearly set a mood|
|Typography|15|A display typeface and a body typeface for each direction, paired well and legible at body size|
|Color system|20|A brand palette and a UI palette for each direction; UI colors come from the brand colors and follow the 60-30-10 proportion|
|Contrast|15|Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast, and button labels are readable on the accent color|
|Application|25|Desktop and mobile screens use the chosen direction consistently (color by role, type, corners), bonus if they align to a grid|
|Explanation|10|2 to 3 clear sentences that connect your design choices to the product, person, or research.|
|Total|100||
