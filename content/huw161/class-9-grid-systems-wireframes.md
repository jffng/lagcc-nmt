### Demo: layout grids

**A grid is the invisible structure behind a page.**

- **Columns:** the vertical stripes content sits in
- **Gutter:** the gap between columns
- **Margin:** the space between the grid and the edge of the screen
- When everything lines up to the same columns, the page looks organized without anyone noticing why

**Set up your grid:**

1. Press **F**, then pick **Desktop** in the right panel (1440 wide)
2. Under **Layout guide** (called **Layout grid** in some versions), click **+**
3. Click the grid icon → change **Grid** to **Columns**
4. Set **Count** 12, **Type** Stretch, **Margin** 120, **Gutter** 24
5. **Ctrl + G** shows and hides the grid. It never shows up in an export
6. Rename the frame **Desktop**

**Spans:** decide how many columns each thing covers.

| Columns       | Looks like                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 12            | Full-width banner, footer            |
| 6 + 6         | Text on one side, image on the other |
| 4 + 4 + 4     | Three cards in a row                 |
| 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 | Four short items in a row            |

**Build a skeleton on the grid:**

1. **R** → draw a rectangle. Drag its edges and they snap to the columns
2. Header: one full-width strip across the top
3. Hero: headline box on columns 1–6, image box on columns 7–12
4. Three cards: 4 columns each
5. Call-to-action strip, 12 columns
6. Footer, 12 columns
7. Duplicate with **Ctrl/⌘ + D**

**Wireframe rules:**

- Grayscale only: grays, black, white
- Image = gray box with the word "Image"
- Real words from your partner, not "lorem ipsum"
- One main button. A visitor should know the one thing to do
- Bigger and darker = noticed first
