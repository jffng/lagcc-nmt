### Part 1: Wireframes

A **wireframe** is a plain black-and-white plan of a page, before any color or pictures.

Requirements:

- Two frames: **computer** (1440px wide) and **phone** (about 390px wide)
	- Black and white, no pictures or color
	- Shows functionality, not design
	- Annotation for what each thing does
- Navigation
- Demonstrates key purpose or action to take

### Part 2: Visual Designs

- Start from your wireframes. Add color, fonts, and pictures
- Two frames again: computer and phone
- - **Short explanation** (2–3 sentences), turned in with your frames:
	- One choice you made on purpose, and why
	- One thing you changed after getting feedback

### How you'll be graded (100 points)

**Part 1: 50 points**

| Category                                           | Excellent                                                        | Proficient                                                | Needs Improvement                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Both versions made** (15 pts)                    | Computer and phone are both complete, with every section         | Both exist, but one is missing sections                   | One version missing, or mostly empty              |
| **Easy to follow** (15 pts)                        | Your eye goes to the most important thing first. Things line up  | Mostly clear. A few things are crowded or off             | Everything looks equally important, or it's messy |
| **Your partner shows up in your choices** (20 pts) | At least 2 choices clearly come from something your partner said | 1 choice, or the notes are vague ("because they like it") | Nothing connects to what your partner said        |

**Part 2: 50 points**

| Category | Excellent | Proficient | Needs Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your partner shows up in your choices** (15 pts) | At least 3 choices (words, pictures, colors, layout) clearly come from something your partner said, each with a "Because" note | 1–2 choices, or the notes are vague | Could be about anyone |
| **Easy to follow** (10 pts) | A visitor can tell what it is and what to do within a few seconds. Everything lines up. Spacing is even | Mostly clear. A few things are crowded or off | Everything looks equally important, or it's messy |
| **Computer + phone** (10 pts) | Both finished. The phone version is rearranged for a small screen and easy to read | Both exist, but the phone version is just a smaller copy | One is missing, or the phone version is hard to read |
| **Looks good** (10 pts) | Fonts, colors, and pictures chosen on purpose. Text is easy to read | Consistent, but some choices feel like defaults | Hard to read, clashing, or unfinished |
| **Short explanation** (5 pts) | A specific choice and why. A real change after feedback | Mentions both, but the reasons are vague | Too vague ("I made it look nice"), missing a part, or not turned in |

Excellent = 90–100% of the points. Proficient = 70–89%. Needs Improvement = under 70%.
