### Today's goal

- **Assignment 2 is due at the end of class today.**
- Finish your **desktop and mobile wireframes**, check them against the checklist, and hand them in.
- A **wireframe** is a plain plan of a page: what goes where, and what each thing does. No visual design yet.

### Checklist: what "done" looks like

- [ ] **Initial sketch + top three goals:** one or two sentences on what the app or site is, and the three most important things it has to help someone do
- [ ] **Mobile wireframe,** about 400px wide
- [ ] **Desktop wireframe,** about 1440px wide
- [ ] **User research:** the research you did for your partner. Any of these works:
  - A link to your Figma file
  - An exported image of your research board
  - A written summary document

### Work time: finish desktop wireframes

- Reread your top three goals. Does your desktop wireframe help with each one?
- Start from your mobile wireframe. Most of the content is already decided.
- The same content gets more room on desktop. Put it on a grid.
- Use your components (buttons, cards, nav) so you aren't redrawing them.
- Add short notes on what things do ("Button: opens the booking form").
- Short on time? Do these first: the part that serves goal #1, nav, then notes.

### Partner review

- Is the app or website's purpose clear?
- What is the first thing you notice? 
- Are all the requirements met? 
- What would make you want to use it more? 

### Break

### Work time: revise + package

- Make your changes from the review.
- Go through the checklist one more time.
- Put your concept, top three goals, sketch, wireframes, and research in one place so they're easy to find (one Figma page works).
- **Export** each wireframe as a PNG: select the frame, open **Export** in the right panel, click **+**, choose **PNG**, then click Export.
- **Figma link:** Share → set to "anyone with the link can view" → Copy link.
- Quick test: open the link in a private window. Can you see it?

### Submit

- Submit what the checklist lists: concept + goals, sketch, both wireframes, and your research.
- If you're not done, submit what you have. Partial work is better than nothing.

### How you'll be graded (100 points)

- **Did you meet the requirements?** (30)
- **Do the wireframes support your top three goals?** (25)
- **Is it clear what the app is trying to do?** (25)
- **Does something from your research show up in a decision?** (20)

### Share

- Last 5 minutes: say or write on a sticky **one thing in your wireframe you're proud of**.
- Pick something specific ("my nav is clear," not "it's fine").
