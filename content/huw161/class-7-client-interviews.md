Client Research

- Pairs: 2 rounds, 20 min each. Swap after round 1.
- Trios: 3 rounds, 15 min each. One person answers, the other two ask and take notes.

Take notes in a new FigJam file. You can use this as a template / copy it to a new FigJam file: https://www.figma.com/board/HNI7QpOuCMbBmLtIo68u5q/User-Research-Template?node-id=0-1&t=RX78o6tAyDdTsx8C-1

**The idea**
1. Tell me about your yellow sticky. What made you think of it?
2. Describe a specific time you wished this existed. What were you doing?

**Motivations**
3. What would having this give you? How would it feel to use it?
4. Who would you share it with or show it to?

**Today**
5. How do you do this now, if at all? An app, a notebook, asking someone, or nothing?
6. What's missing, or what gets in the way?

**Imagine it**
7. Describe your ideal version. What's the first thing you'd do with it?
8. Is there an app, site, game, or place that already feels a little like this? What do you like about it?
9. What would make it feel like it's not for you?

**Follow-up questions to any of the above**
- "Can you give me an example?"
- "What happened next?"
- "Why does that matter to you?"

**Lightning round** (10 min, quick answers)

**Basics** (all optional)
- Do you work, or are you a full time student?? What are you studying?
- What's your commute like?
- What device are you on most, and for what?

**Sliders:**
- **Introvert ↔ Extrovert:** After a long week, how do you recharge?
- **Analytical ↔ Creative:** Planning a trip, do you make a list or go with a feeling?
- **Busy ↔ Time-rich:** How many free hours do you actually have in a week?
- **Messy ↔ Organized:** Can I see your phone's home screen?
- **Independent ↔ Team player:** In a group project, do you lead, take a piece, or wish you could work alone?
- **Passive ↔ Active:** On social media, do you mostly scroll, comment, or post?
- **Safe ↔ Risky:** At your favorite place, do you order the same thing or try something new?

Each answer places a dot on that slider in your persona.
