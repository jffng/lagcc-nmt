### Demo: Figma image adjustments

Figma can edit photos with a set of sliders. Each slider does something that
photo editors have done for decades.

**To find them:** select your photo → in the right panel under **Fill**,
click the small image thumbnail. The adjustment sliders appear in the panel
that opens.

| Slider | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Exposure** | Brightens or darkens the whole image |
| **Contrast** | Pushes darks darker and lights lighter (or flattens them together) |
| **Saturation** | How strong the colors are. All the way down (−100) = black and white |
| **Temperature** | Warmer (orange) or cooler (blue) |
| **Tint** | Greener or more magenta. Fixes weird color casts from indoor lights |
| **Highlights** | Adjusts only the brightest parts |
| **Shadows** | Adjusts only the darkest parts |

**Non-destructive editing:** the sliders never change your original photo.
You can reset any slider back to 0 at any time. Professional editors work this
way, so you never lose the original.

**Crop, rotate, and flip:**

- **Crop:** in the same image panel, change the dropdown from **Fill** to
  **Crop**, then drag the edges.
- **Straighten:** type a small angle into the rotation field in the right
  panel (like 2° or −3°) until the horizon or face looks level.
- **Flip:** **Shift + H** (horizontal) or **Shift + V** (vertical).

### Assignment 2: Mini-Comic or Photo Essay

Due Thursday, Oct 22

**Both**
- 3–8 frames (~5 is a good target)
- Same size for every frame: story 1080 × 1920 or carousel 1080 × 1350
- Beginning, middle, end
- Text required: captions or speech bubbles
- A frame can hold more than one image

**Comic**
- Characters + something happens
- Original imagery: drawn (Figma or any drawing app) or staged photos you take
- Existing characters OK, but draw something new in every frame
- Retelling an existing story is fine

**Photo essay**
- About something real
- Title frame + a caption on every frame
- You must credit any photos you did not take
- Anyone recognizable agreed to be photographed

### Content guidelines

Hard subjects are OK

**Not tolerated:**
- Hate and harassment
- Targeting real people, including classmates
- Sexually explicit content
- Gratuitous violence or gore
- Photos taken without consent
- Wholly AI-generated images

Are you choosing a potentially difficult subject?
- Check with me first
- Add a content note on your first frame
- You never have to explain the personal side

### Using images you didn't make

- Photo essay: your own photos only, or photos used with permission
- Comic: borrowed images only as reference or one transformed collage piece
- Credit anything you borrow
- Posting publicly? Use only your own or free-to-use images
